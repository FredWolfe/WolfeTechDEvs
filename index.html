About Me

Who I Am

ALX Africa • Data Engineering

Hi, my name is Wolfe Isaac Kweku Awotwi. I have a background in Information Technology Management, and I'm currently specializing in Data Engineering through ALX.

I have experience in mobile and full-stack development using Flutter and the MERN stack, along with practical exposure to network systems and infrastructure. I've contributed to building an e-commerce platform as part of a team, and I've also led and mentored a group to develop a fully functional service platform with mobile applications and an administrative dashboard.

My core strengths include data collection and cleaning, building scalable applications, and solving real-world problems through technology. I'm especially interested in using technology and data-driven systems to support job creation and improve access to opportunities within local communities.

What sets me apart is my versatility across different areas of tech, my ability to lead and collaborate effectively, and my drive to continuously learn and take on challenging tasks. I enjoy understanding how systems work at a deeper level and building solutions that can scale and create meaningful impact.

I'm particularly interested in working with tech-driven organizations — especially in data, backend systems, or scalable platforms — where I can contribute to building impactful solutions that improve lives and solve real-world problems.

Beyond tech, I love movies, games, and hanging out with friends. I've also always had an interest in acting and animation, and I enjoy bringing creativity into the way I approach problem-solving and innovation.
