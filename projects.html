AHICP — Insurance Risk Analytics & BI Platform

A full end-to-end insurance risk analytics build simulating an internal data analytics team: raw Excel data profiled and cleaned in Python, loaded into Google BigQuery, transformed with SQL feature engineering into a custom 0–100 Risk Score per employee, and delivered as three interactive Power BI dashboards (Executive, Customer Risk, Operations).

17 business questions answered end-to-end, including a caught-and-fixed data quality bug (an INNER JOIN silently dropping 558 employees) that materially changed the final risk picture — a real example of analyst rigor, not just chart building.
